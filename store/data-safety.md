# Play Console: Data safety and app content answers

Paste into **Play Console → Policy → App content**. Matches the privacy policy section `#prism-pop`.

## Data safety
- **Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?** Yes (only if the player turns on sync).
- **Is all user data encrypted in transit?** Yes (HTTPS).
- **Do you provide a way for users to request that their data be deleted?** Yes: by email or GitHub issue, linked in the privacy policy.
- **Data types:** App activity → *Other user-generated content* (encrypted game progress).
  - Collected: Yes · Shared: No · Processed ephemerally: No
  - Required or optional: **Optional** (sync is off by default)
  - Purpose: **App functionality** (same progress on the player's other devices)
- Not collected: location, personal info, contacts, device IDs, advertising ID, analytics, crash logs.

## Other app content
- **Ads:** No ads.
- **Target audience (recommended):** 13+ (keep out of the Families programme; sync uses a network service).
- **Content rating (IARC):** Puzzle, no violence, no user-to-user chat, no purchases → expected *Everyone / PEGI 3*.
- **Government app / financial features / health:** No.
- **Privacy policy URL:** https://sabin78910.github.io/privacy-policy/#prism-pop
